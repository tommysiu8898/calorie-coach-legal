# Coach avatars — source

Custom 3D characters generated with **Google Gemini** (Gemini app, image
generation) in October 2026 from the brief in `PROMPTS.md`. Google does not
claim ownership of generated images; they are used here under the Gemini
terms of service.

Each coach was generated as ONE 3×2 character sheet (same character in six
poses), then cut into separate images: white background removed, cropped to
the same head size and position, resized to 240×240 and saved as 256-colour
PNG; a few coaches were then zoomed slightly so every face is the same size. The heart-hands pose of some coaches reuses the face from the same
coach's thumbs-up pose, because the generator kept drawing it with worried
eyebrows.
