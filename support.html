CalSpark Support

We're here to help. Email us at support@calspark.cloud and we'll reply within 2 business days. Please tell us what happened and, if you can, include a screenshot.

Report a problem with community content

In any group chat, tap the ⋯ next to a message (or press and hold it) to Report it or Block its author. Blocking removes that person's messages from your feed immediately. Our team reviews every report within 24 hours and removes content and users that break our Terms of Use. You can also email support@calspark.cloud.

Manage or cancel your subscription

CalSpark Premium is billed by Apple. Open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → CalSpark to change or cancel it. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore a purchase

On the subscription screen, tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and data. Deleting the app alone does not cancel a subscription.

How calories and targets are calculated

In the app, open Profile → Sources & methods to see each formula and the research it comes from. CalSpark is a general wellness tool, not medical advice.

Privacy requests

For access, correction or deletion requests, email privacy@calspark.cloud. See our Privacy Policy.
