FAQ

Questions, answered

How accurate are the calorie estimates?

Photo estimates are AI approximations — usually close for common dishes, less certain for mixed or hidden ingredients. You can adjust any portion or item before logging.

Where do my targets and health numbers come from?

From published research: WHO BMI categories, the Mifflin–St Jeor equation for resting metabolism, FAO/WHO/UNU activity levels and more. Every source is listed in the app under Profile → Sources & methods.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. With your permission, CalSpark reads steps, workouts, active and resting energy, sleep and heart rate to keep your daily budget accurate.

Which languages are supported?

English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, French, German, Spanish and Italian.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CalSpark. See our Support page for more.

Is CalSpark medical advice?

No. CalSpark is a general wellness and self-tracking tool, not a medical device. Please talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before major changes to your diet.
